Sources, coverage & how to read this graph

Connections come from OpenAlex. Retraction status comes from Crossref update notices. “No notice found” means no update notice was returned by this lookup; it does not establish that a paper is reliable. Unknown means the lookup could not be completed or no DOI was available. OpenAlex flags are shown separately when they disagree.

Citation paths show relationships, not whether a paper endorses the cited work. Dates and status reflect the displayed snapshot or cached lookup. This demo does not analyze full text or determine whether a citation occurred before or after retraction.
